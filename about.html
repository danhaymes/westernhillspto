Who we are

About the PTO

The Western Hills Elementary PTO is a volunteer group of parents, guardians, teachers and staff working together for every student at Western Hills.

Our purpose

What we're here for

We build community among Western Hills families, support our teachers and staff, and raise money for the programs and extras that make the school year memorable.

If your child attends Western Hills, or you teach or work here, you're part of the PTO. Come to a meeting, help at an event, or just say hello.
