Common questions

Who is part of the PTO?

Every parent, guardian, teacher and staff member at Western Hills. You don't need to sign up to come to a meeting or volunteer.

Do I have to come to every meeting?

No. Come when you can. Meetings are a good way to hear what's planned and share ideas.

How do I volunteer?

Sign up for an open shift on the Get Involved page, or email us and we'll match you with something that fits your schedule.

What does the PTO spend money on?

Funds go to student programs, family events, classroom and staff support, and school projects. Budget questions are always welcome at general meetings.

How can I suggest an idea?

Bring it to a general meeting or email westernhillspto@gmail.com. We love hearing from families.
